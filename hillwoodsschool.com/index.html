    Welcome to Hillwoods School

    Established in the year 2001, Hillwoods School, Gandhinagar, is affiliated with the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), New Delhi, and prepares students for the All India Senior Secondary School Examination. A branch of Hillwoods Academy, Delhi (established in 1982), the school has consistently upheld a tradition of academic excellence and holistic development.

    With a strong foundation in academics, sports, and co-curricular activities, Hillwoods School provides a nurturing environment that fosters intellectual growth, creativity, and leadership. The school is committed to 100% board exam results year after year, empowering students to achieve their highest potential.

    Our state-of-the-art infrastructure includes well-equipped laboratories for Science, Mathematics, and Computer Science, a vast library catering to diverse interests, and spacious sports grounds for Basketball, Volleyball, Football, Cricket, and Athletics. We integrate modern teaching methodologies with technology-enabled learning, ensuring a dynamic and engaging educational experience.

    Our state-of-the-art infrastructure includes well-equipped laboratories for Science, Mathematics, and Computer Science, a vast library catering to diverse interests, and spacious sports grounds for Basketball, Volleyball, Football, Cricket, and Athletics. We integrate modern teaching methodologies with technology-enabled learning, ensuring a dynamic and engaging educational experience.

    To keep pace with the evolving world, we provide AI training to teachers and students, enabling them to incorporate artificial intelligence into the teaching-learning process. This initiative prepares our students for the future, equipping them with essential 21st-century skills.

    At Hillwoods, we believe in shaping individuals of strong character, high principles, and a compassionate outlook—preparing them to excel in an ever-evolving world.
